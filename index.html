01 — about

A little about me

I got into CS because of video games. The joy they gave me is something I want to recreate for other people, and wanting to understand how games actually worked sent me down a rabbit hole that never really ended.

Courses like Assembly and DSA helped me realize I’m especially interested in how software executes at a lower level and how systems are designed. That interest grew further in my Software Engineering course, where exposure to a professor who previously worked on medical devices made me curious about how software is built for real world software systems. That curiosity led me toward systems programming, embedded software, and backend work, areas of CS focused on how software actually runs.

Outside of code, I lift weights, snowboard, and tend to pick up new interests quickly.
